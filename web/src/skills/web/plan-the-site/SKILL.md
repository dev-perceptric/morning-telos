# Plan the site

A page list for the website that matches how customers search, in the order to build it.

## When to use
- A new or rebuilt website.
- The owner asks which pages are missing.

## When not to use
- They want one page written. Use write-a-page.

## What you have
- search_knowledge: services, areas and the pages already on the site.
- keyword_research: how people search for each service. 10 tasks a call, at most two calls.
- get_topic_bank and get_content_plan: ideas and pages already planned.
- add_to_content_plan: saves the new pages to the plan. It waits for approval.

## Steps
1. List the services and areas from Knowledge, and the pages the site already has.
2. Check the content plan and topic bank so nothing is planned twice.
3. Research one or two seed keywords to see how people name each service.
4. Plan the pages: homepage, one page per main service, one per main area if the business travels, about, contact or booking, and an FAQ.
5. Give each page its keyword, its job and the call to book.
6. Offer to add the new pages to the content plan with add_to_content_plan.

## Output
- The page list: page, keyword, job, in build order.
- The homepage sections in one short list.

## Rules
- Keep it to pages a small business can keep up. Ten pages or fewer to start.
