# Write a campaign

One email campaign, written in the business's voice and ready for the owner to approve.

## When to use
- The owner has one thing to tell their customers by email.

## When not to use
- The email is for people who just signed up or bought. Use welcome-series.
- The email is for people who stopped buying. Use win-back.

## What you have
- search_knowledge: the real details (dates, prices, address), the offer and the business's tone.
- app_search_actions: shows whether Gmail or Outlook is connected. The campaign goes out from that inbox.
- mailing_lists: the business's lists and how many people on each can get email.
- save_campaign: saves the campaign as a draft on your Campaigns tab. Nothing is sent until the owner approves it there.
- remember: saves what the owner likes, such as a sign-off or a tone, for next time.

## Steps
1. Read the details and the tone with search_knowledge. Ask for the one fact that is missing, if any.
2. Check who can get it with mailing_lists. Ask which list if the owner didn't say. If the list is empty, say to import contacts in your Settings tab.
3. Write three subject lines and pick the best one. Write the email: a first line that says why it matters, the details, and one clear ask. Start with "Hi {{first_name}},".
4. Save it with save_campaign. Leave out the unsubscribe line and the address: they're added to every email.
5. Check the inbox with app_search_actions. If neither Gmail nor Outlook is connected, say to connect one in Settings, Apps before it can go out.
6. Tell the owner it's on your Campaigns tab, where they can send a test to themselves and approve it.
7. If the owner corrects the tone or the sign-off, save it with remember.

## Output
- The subject line and the email, ready to read.
- Which list gets it and how many people.
- One line saying it's on the Campaigns tab waiting for their OK.

## Rules
- Never invent an offer, a price, a date or a result.
- Email only people who know the business or asked to hear from it. Never a bought list.
- Never send a campaign one email at a time with the email app. It goes out from the Campaigns tab.
