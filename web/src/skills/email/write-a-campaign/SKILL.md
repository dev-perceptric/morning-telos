# Write a campaign

One email campaign, written in the business's voice and ready for the owner to approve.

## When to use
- The owner has one thing to tell their customers by email.

## When not to use
- The email is for people who just signed up or bought. Use welcome-series.
- The email is for people who stopped buying. Use win-back.

## What you have
- search_knowledge: the real details (dates, prices, address), the offer and the business's tone.
- app_search_actions: shows the connected email app and its actions, like sending an email or saving a draft.
- app_action: sends the email or saves a draft. Sending waits for the owner's approval.
- remember: saves what the owner likes, such as a sign-off or a tone, for next time.

## Steps
1. Read the details and the tone with search_knowledge. Ask for the one fact that is missing, if any.
2. Ask who it goes to if the owner didn't say. Use only addresses the owner gives or that are already in their email app.
3. Write three subject lines and pick the best one. Write the email: a first line that says why it matters, the details, and one clear ask.
4. Check the email app with app_search_actions. If none is connected, show the email in chat and say to connect Gmail or Outlook in Settings, Apps.
5. When the owner says it looks right, send it with app_action, one email per person, or save it as a draft.
6. If the owner corrects the tone or the sign-off, save it with remember.

## Output
- The subject line and the email, ready to read.
- Who it goes to.
- One line saying it waits for approval, or that it is in chat because no email app is connected.

## Rules
- Never invent an offer, a price, a date or a result.
- Email only people who know the business or asked to hear from it. Never a bought list.
- For more than 20 people, write the campaign and say it is best sent from an email marketing tool.
