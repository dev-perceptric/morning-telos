Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Plain-English summary: we collect only what we need to run your AI team, we never sell your data, we don't train AI on it, and you can delete it anytime.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, business name, website and time zone.
  • What you put in the app: chats, knowledge docs, routines and approvals.
  • Usage: how much AI your workspace uses, so we can show your allowance and bill correctly.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We see your plan and payment status, not your full card number.
  • Security logs: sign-in attempts and errors, to keep the app safe.

How we use it

  • To run your AI team and the services we operate for your business.
  • To send account emails, like email verification, password resets and billing notices.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

We don't sell your data, we don't use it for ads, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Who helps us run the service

  • Anthropic (Claude): the AI models your team uses.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Hostinger: our servers.
  • Resend: account emails.
  • Cloudflare: encrypted backups.
  • Arrowsterr and Capybaroar: SEO, AI visibility and social media tools we run for your business.
  • Google Fonts: fonts on our pages.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed. Service keys are encrypted.
  • Each business's data is kept separate, and your AI team can only reach your own brand in the services we run.
  • Backups are encrypted and kept for 30 days.

How long we keep it

As long as your workspace exists. When you delete it, we delete your data from live systems right away and from backups within 30 days. Billing records may be kept longer where the law requires.

Your choices

You can see and edit your information in the app, download what you've written, and delete your workspace in Settings. For anything else, email meet@morningtelos.com.

Cookies

The app uses one cookie to keep you logged in. Our website doesn't use tracking cookies.

The Chrome extension

  • Only on your click: it reads a web page only when you click a button or a right-click item in it, never in the background.
  • What it sends: what you chose to share (the page's address, title and words, text you selected, a screenshot if you asked for one, or the open email in Gmail) goes to your own workspace, where your AI team uses it to answer you.
  • What we keep: page text is saved with your chat, like any message, and is deleted with it. Screenshots are used for that one answer and are not stored.
  • What it never reads: pages with a password box, bank and payment pages, password managers and Chrome's own pages.
  • What it never does: it never clicks, submits or sends anything on a page. You send every email yourself.
  • Sign-in: you sign in on chat.morningtelos.com. The extension keeps its sign-in in its own storage, and Sign out removes it.
