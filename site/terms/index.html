Terms of Service

Last updated November 17, 2026

Plain-English summary: you own your business data, your AI team only acts inside your workspace, changes wait for your approval unless you turn on Autopilot, and you can cancel or delete your workspace anytime.

1. Who we are

Morning Telos ("we", "us") runs the Morning Telos app at chat.morningtelos.com and the website at morningtelos.com. You can reach us at meet@morningtelos.com.

2. Your account

  • You must give a real email address and keep your password safe.
  • You must be at least 18 and able to make agreements for your business.
  • The person who creates a workspace is its owner. The owner can add and remove teammates, change the plan and delete the workspace.
  • You are responsible for what happens in your workspace, including what your teammates do.

3. Free trial and paid plans

  • New workspaces get a 14-day free trial. No card is needed.
  • Paid plans renew every month until you cancel. Payments are handled by Stripe.
  • Each plan includes an AI allowance for each billing period. When it runs out, your team pauses until the next period or until you upgrade.
  • You can cancel anytime in Billing. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. We don't give refunds for partial months unless the law requires it.
  • We may change prices with at least 30 days' notice by email.

4. How the AI team works

  • Your AI employees use AI models to read, write and take actions in the services we run for your business, such as SEO and social media.
  • By default, anything that changes something (publishing, scheduling, sending, deleting) waits for your approval. If you turn on Autopilot for a teammate, it can make changes without asking.
  • AI can make mistakes. Check important work before you approve it. You are responsible for content you approve or publish.
  • Don't use the app to break the law, send spam, mislead people, infringe others' rights or harm anyone.

5. Your content

You own the information you add and the content your team creates for you. You give us permission to store and process it only to run the service for you. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it to train AI models.

6. Availability

We work to keep the app running and back up data every night, but we can't promise it will never go down. We may update the app over time.

7. Ending your account

You can delete your workspace anytime in Settings. That deletes its chats, knowledge, approvals and routines from our live systems right away, and from backups within 30 days. We may suspend accounts that break these terms or don't pay.

8. Liability

The app is provided "as is." To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or lost-profit damages, and our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the 3 months before the claim.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. If a change is important, we'll email the workspace owner before it takes effect.
