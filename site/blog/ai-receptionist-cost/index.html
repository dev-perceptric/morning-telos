How Much Does an AI Receptionist Cost? [2026 Prices]

AI receptionist prices from 13 real providers, what 100 or 300 calls a month really costs, how AI compares with human answering services and in-house staff, and when custom makes sense.

Short answer: An AI receptionist costs about $25 to $500 a month for most small businesses. Plans are priced by calls, minutes or callers, and extra usage is billed on top. Human answering services start around $250 to $300 a month for a small number of calls, and an in-house receptionist costs a median $38,010 a year in pay alone. Custom AI receptionists cost $5,000 to $25,000 to build.

What changes the price

  • Call volume and length: The biggest factor. Long calls hurt on per-minute plans, and many short calls hurt on per-call plans.
  • Pricing unit: Calls, minutes, unique callers and conversations are all counted differently. Compare at your real volume.
  • Overage rates: Going over your plan can cost $0.15 to $3 per call or $0.25 to $0.50 per minute on AI apps.
  • Integrations: Booking into your calendar, CRM or practice software may need a higher plan or setup work.
  • Phone costs: Voice AI platforms like Retell AI and Bland AI bill phone lines and numbers separately.
  • Human backup: Hybrid plans that hand calls to a live person cost more than AI-only plans.

Buy an app or build custom?

For most small businesses, an AI receptionist app is the right start. A custom build makes sense when the receptionist has to book into your own software, follow rules no app supports, or handle enough calls that per-call fees add up.

FAQ

How much does an AI receptionist cost per month?

Most AI receptionist apps for small businesses cost about $25 to $500 a month, depending on how many calls or minutes you need. As of November 2026, Upfirst starts at $24.95 a month, Rosie at $49, RingCentral's AI Receptionist at $49, Goodcall at $79 and Smith.ai's AI Receptionist Pro at $150.

Is an AI receptionist cheaper than a human receptionist?

Usually, yes. An AI receptionist app often costs $50 to $500 a month. Human answering services like Ruby and Smith.ai's human receptionists start around $250 to $300 a month for a small number of calls or minutes and climb quickly. An in-house receptionist costs a median $38,010 a year in pay alone.

Do AI receptionists charge per call or per minute?

Both models exist. Smith.ai and Upfirst charge by calls, Goodcall by unique callers, Rosie, RingCentral and Dialzara by minutes, and Nextiva by conversations. Voice AI platforms like Retell AI and Bland AI charge per minute plus separate phone costs.

Is there a free AI receptionist?

Smith.ai has a free AI Receptionist plan with 25 calls a month, and Bland AI has a free tier with per-minute charges. Several others offer free trials, such as Rosie (7 days) and Upfirst (14 days).

When does a custom AI receptionist make sense?

When the receptionist must book into your own scheduling or practice software, follow unusual rules, handle very high call volume, or take actions in several systems. Custom builds usually cost $5,000 to $25,000 up front plus a monthly fee, so they pay off mostly for larger or multi-location businesses.
