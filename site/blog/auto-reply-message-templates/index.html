40+ Auto Reply Message Templates for Small Business

Copy-and-paste auto reply messages for texts, email, after hours, holidays, social DMs, new leads and website chat.

Short answer: A good auto reply thanks the customer, says when they'll hear back and gives one next step. For example: "Thanks for texting [Business]! We'll reply within an hour. Want to book now? Pick a time here: [Link]."

Customers text, email and DM at all hours. When nobody answers, many of them move on to the next business on the list.

An auto reply fixes the first few minutes. It tells the customer you got their message and keeps them from shopping around while you finish what you're doing.

Below are 45 auto reply templates you can copy and paste. They cover texts, email, after hours, holidays, social DMs, new leads and website chat.

What makes a good auto reply message?

  • Say thanks first: a short "thanks for reaching out" sets a friendly tone.
  • Confirm you got it: people want to know their message didn't vanish.
  • Set a clear time: "within an hour" or "by 9am tomorrow" beats "soon."
  • Give one next step: a booking link, a phone number or a quick question to answer.
  • Keep it short: two or three sentences for texts and DMs. Email can be a bit longer.
  • Sound like you: use your business name and, where it fits, a real staff name.

45 auto reply message templates

Swap the words in square brackets for your own details. Keep the time frames honest, so customers trust the next message too.

Text message auto replies

General text auto reply

Hi, thanks for texting [Business]! We got your message and will reply within [time frame]. If it's urgent, call us at [Phone].

Busy with a customer

Thanks for your text! We're with a customer right now and will get back to you by [Time]. - [Staff name], [Business]

With a booking link

Hi! Thanks for reaching out to [Business]. Want to book faster? Pick a time here: [Link]. Or just wait and we'll text you back shortly.

Quick menu

Thanks for texting [Business]! So we can help faster, reply with a number: 1 = Book an appointment 2 = Get a quote 3 = Something else

On a job or driving

Hi, this is [Staff name] at [Business]. I'm on a job and can't text right now. I'll reply as soon as I'm done, usually within [time frame].

Email out-of-office replies

Standard out of office

Subject: Out of office until [Date] Hi, Thanks for your email. I'm out of the office until [Date] and will have limited access to email. For anything urgent, please contact [Staff name] at [Email] or [Phone]. I'll reply when I'm back. [Name] [Business]

Short and simple

Thanks for your message. I'm away until [Date] and will reply when I return. For urgent questions, please call [Phone].

Shared team inbox

Hi there, Thanks for contacting [Business]. This is an automatic reply to let you know we got your email. Our team answers messages [Day] to [Day], [Time] to [Time]. You'll hear from us within one business day. Need to book? You can pick a time here: [Link] Thanks, The [Business] team

Traveling or at an event

Hi, I'm at [event] through [Date], so my replies will be slower than usual. I'll check email once a day. If you need help right away, please reach out to [Staff name] at [Email]. Thanks for your patience, [Name]

Extended leave

Hi, I'm on leave until [Date] and won't be checking email. Your message has not been forwarded. For help in the meantime, please contact [Staff name] at [Email]. They know my clients and projects and can help you. Thank you, [Name]

After-hours auto replies

After-hours text

Thanks for texting [Business]! We're closed right now. Our hours are [Day] to [Day], [Time] to [Time]. We'll reply first thing when we open.

After hours with booking link

Hi! We're closed for the night, but you can book online any time: [Link]. If you'd rather talk, we'll call you back after [Time] tomorrow.

After hours with emergency line

Thanks for reaching out to [Business]. Our office is closed. If this is an emergency, call our after-hours line at [Phone]. For everything else, we'll reply when we open at [Time].

After-hours email

Hi, Thanks for your email. Our office is closed right now. Our team will reply during business hours, [Day] to [Day], [Time] to [Time]. If you need to book, change or cancel an appointment, you can do that here: [Link] Thanks, [Business]

Weekend

Thanks for your message! We're closed for the weekend and will be back [Day] at [Time]. We'll get back to you then. Have a great weekend!

Holiday and closure auto replies

Holiday closure

Happy holidays from [Business]! We're closed from [Date] to [Date]. We'll reply to your message when we're back on [Date].

One-day holiday

Thanks for texting [Business]! We're closed today for [holiday]. We'll be back tomorrow at [Time] and will reply then.

Holiday hours

Thanks for reaching out! Our hours change this week for the holiday: [Day]: [Time] to [Time] [Day]: Closed We'll reply as soon as we're in. Book any time at [Link].

Year-end closure email

Hi, Thanks for your email. Our office is closed for the holidays from [Date] through [Date]. We'll answer messages in the order we got them when we're back on [Date]. For urgent matters, please call [Phone]. Wishing you a happy new year, The [Business] team

Unexpected closure

Hi, [Business] is closed today, [Date], because of [reason]. If you had an appointment, we'll text you to reschedule. Sorry for the trouble!

Booked solid and busy season replies

Fully booked this week

Thanks for reaching out! We're fully booked this week. The next open spot is [Day] at [Time]. Want it? Reply YES and we'll hold it for you.

Waitlist

Hi! We're booked up right now, but we can add you to our waitlist. Reply WAIT and we'll text you if a spot opens up.

Busy season

Thanks for contacting [Business]. It's our busy season, so replies may take up to [time frame]. To skip the wait, book online here: [Link].

Not taking new clients right now

Thanks so much for your interest in [Business]. We're not taking new clients right now. We'd be glad to let you know when spots open up. Just reply YES.

Slower replies email

Hi, Thanks for your message. We're getting more requests than usual, so it may take us up to [time frame] to reply. The fastest way to book is online: [Link] We appreciate your patience. [Business]

Instagram and Facebook DM auto replies

Meta Business Suite lets you set an instant reply and an away message for both inboxes.

Instagram instant reply

Hey, thanks for messaging [Business]! We usually reply within a few hours. Want to book now? Tap here: [Link]

Facebook away message

Thanks for your message! We're away right now and will reply when we're back at [Time]. You can also call us at [Phone].

Price question in DMs

Thanks for asking about pricing! Most [Service] jobs run [price range], depending on [factor]. Want an exact quote? Send us a few details and we'll reply soon.

Comment reply that moves to DMs

Thanks for the question, [Name]! We just sent you a DM with the details.

Story reply about an offer

Thanks for replying to our story! The [offer] runs until [Date]. Want us to save you a spot? Reply with the day and time that works for you.

Google, Yelp and other listing messages

Google Business Profile no longer has its own chat inbox, so most Google leads reach you by phone, website or text. Yelp and other directories still have message inboxes. Use these for leads from any listing.

Yelp quote request

Hi [Name], thanks for your request on Yelp! We got your details about [Service]. A team member will reply with a quote within [time frame]. Questions in the meantime? Call [Phone].

Missed call from your Google listing

Hi, this is [Business]. Sorry we missed your call! Were you calling about [Service]? Reply here and we'll help, or book online: [Link]

Nextdoor or directory message

Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out on [platform]! We'd be glad to help with [Service]. What's the best number to reach you?

Service area check

Hi [Name], thanks for your message about [Service]. We serve [area], so you're in the right place. We'll reply with next steps within [time frame].

New lead instant replies

Speed matters most here. Someone who just filled out your form is often contacting other businesses at the same time.

Web form reply (text)

Hi [Name], this is [Staff name] from [Business]. Thanks for your request! I'll call you within [time frame]. Is now a good time, or is later today better?

Web form reply (email)

Subject: We got your request, [Name] Hi [Name], Thanks for contacting [Business] about [Service]. We got your request and [Staff name] will reach out by [Day]. Want to skip the back and forth? Book a time that works for you here: [Link] Talk soon, [Staff name] [Business] | [Phone]

Quote request

Hi [Name], thanks for requesting a quote from [Business]! To get it right, can you send a photo of [item or area]? We'll have your quote ready by [Day].

Book a call

Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! The fastest way to talk is to grab a time on our calendar: [Link]. Talk soon, [Staff name]

Lead from an ad

Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in our [offer]! Want to claim it? Reply YES and we'll get you booked. Reply STOP to opt out.

Next-day nudge

Hi [Name], just checking in from [Business]. Still need help with [Service]? Reply here or book a time: [Link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

Website chat greetings and replies

Chat greeting

Hi there! Welcome to [Business]. What can we help you with today?

Chat when you're offline

Thanks for stopping by! We're offline right now. Leave your name and phone number or email, and we'll get back to you by [Time] on the next business day.

Handoff to a person

Thanks! I'm passing this to [Staff name] on our team. They'll reply here in a few minutes. If you need to step away, leave your email and we'll follow up.

Chat with quick options

Hi! How can we help today? - Book an appointment - Get a quote - Ask a question - Talk to a person

Pricing page prompt

Questions about pricing? We're happy to help you pick the right option. Ask away and we'll answer right here.

Tips and mistakes to avoid

  • Don't promise times you can't keep. If you say "within an hour," reply within an hour.
  • Turn off holiday replies when you're back. An old "we're closed" message makes you look closed for good.
  • Give urgent issues a path. Add a phone number or emergency line for anything that can't wait.
  • Don't stop at the auto reply. Someone still needs to follow up. Set a reminder or route the message to a person.
  • Test every reply. Text, email and DM your own business to see exactly what customers see.

FAQ

What should an auto reply message say?

Thank the person, confirm you got their message and say when they'll hear back. Then give one next step, like a booking link or a phone number for urgent issues.

How fast should an auto reply go out?

Right away. The whole point is to reply within seconds, so the customer knows the message landed and doesn't move on to the next business.

Can I use auto replies for business texts?

Yes. Auto replies to people who texted you first are common. If you send marketing texts, like offers or follow-ups, get the customer's permission first and include a way to opt out, such as Reply STOP.

What is the difference between an auto reply and an AI receptionist?

An auto reply sends the same fixed message every time. An AI receptionist reads the message, answers the question, and can book an appointment or pass the lead to your team.

How do I set up auto replies on Instagram and Facebook?

Meta Business Suite has inbox settings for instant replies and away messages. Paste in one of the templates above, add your booking link, and test it from a personal account.
