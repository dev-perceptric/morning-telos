What makes a good missed call text back?

  • Send it fast: The best time is right after the call ends, while the caller still has their phone in hand.
  • Say who you are: Put your business name in the first line. The caller may not have your number saved.
  • Say sorry, then offer help: A short "Sorry we missed your call" sets a friendly tone.
  • Give one next step: Ask them to reply, share a booking link or promise a callback time. Pick one per text.
  • Match the time of day: A daytime text can promise a quick callback. A night text should say when you'll be back.
  • Keep it short: Aim for two or three short sentences. Long texts get skimmed.

Tips and mistakes to avoid

  • Text from your business number. Replies should come back to the same number the customer called.
  • Promise only what you can keep. If you say "within an hour," call back within an hour.
  • Send one text per caller per day. If the same person calls three times, they should get one text.
  • Send urgent replies to a real phone. Don't let an emergency sit in an inbox until morning.
  • Ask for privacy in sensitive fields. Dental offices, clinics and law firms should ask callers to keep health or case details out of texts.
  • Add "Reply STOP to opt out" to follow-ups. Then honor it right away.
  • Track which texts get replies. Try two versions for a month and keep the one that works better.

FAQ

What is a missed call text back?

It's an automatic text that goes to anyone whose call you didn't answer. It tells them who you are, says sorry and gives them a quick way to keep the conversation going by text.

What should I say when I miss a customer's call?

Say who you are, say sorry and give one next step. For example: Hi, this is [Business]. Sorry we missed your call! How can we help? Reply here and we'll get right back to you.

How fast should a missed call text go out?

As fast as you can, ideally within a minute. The caller is still thinking about the call, and they may be about to try another business.

Is it legal to text people who called my business?

Rules vary by country and state. Most businesses keep the first text about the call itself, don't add callers to marketing lists without permission, and honor STOP replies right away. Ask a lawyer if you're unsure.

What if the caller used a landline?

Landlines usually can't get texts, so the message won't arrive. Some tools can check whether a number can take texts. Either way, call back anyone who doesn't reply.
