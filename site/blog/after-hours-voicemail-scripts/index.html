30+ After-Hours Voicemail Greeting & Text Scripts

Copy-and-paste after-hours voicemail greetings for evenings, weekends, holidays, lunch breaks and emergency lines, plus matching texts to send callers.

Short answer: A good after-hours voicemail greeting says your business name, tells callers you're closed, gives your hours and says when you'll call back. If you offer emergency service, explain how to reach the on-call line, and tell anyone with a life-threatening emergency to call 911. Example: "Hi, you've reached [Business]. We're closed right now. Leave your name and number and we'll call you back tomorrow by [Time]."

Calls don't stop when you lock the door. People call about a leaky pipe at 8 p.m., a sick child on Saturday or a legal problem over a holiday weekend.

This guide is for clinics, law firms, home service companies, property managers and any business that gets calls after hours. You'll find 31 scripts: voicemail greetings for every situation, plus short texts to send callers so they aren't left waiting.

What makes a good after-hours voicemail greeting?

  • Say your business name first. Callers want to know right away that they dialed the right number.
  • Give your hours. Tell people when you open again so they know when to expect a call.
  • Say when you'll call back. "By 10 a.m. tomorrow" is more reassuring than "as soon as possible."
  • Handle emergencies clearly. Tell callers with a life-threatening emergency to hang up and call 911. Give urgent callers your on-call number or a menu option.
  • Keep it short. Aim for 20 to 30 seconds. Put the most important details at the start.
  • Give another way to reach you. A booking link or "text this number" helps callers who won't leave a message.

Standard after-hours greetings

Simple after-hours greeting

Hi, you've reached [Business]. Our office is closed right now. Our hours are [Day] through [Day], [Time] to [Time]. Please leave your name, number and a short message, and we'll call you back the next business day. You can also book online anytime at [Link]. Thanks for calling!

Greeting that promises a text back

Thanks for calling [Business]. We're closed for the day, and we don't want you to wait until morning for an answer. After the beep, leave your name and what you need. We'll text you a link to book, and we'll call you back when we open at [Time].

Short and friendly greeting

Hi, this is [Business]. We're out of the office right now. Leave a message and we'll get back to you first thing tomorrow morning. Thanks!

Greeting for a solo owner

Hi, you've reached [Staff name] at [Business]. I'm done for the day. Leave your name, number and what you need, and I'll call you back tomorrow by [Time]. If it's easier, text this number and I'll reply first thing.

Weekend greetings

Closed for the weekend

Open Saturday, closed Sunday

Hi, you've reached [Business]. We're closed right now. Our weekend hours are Saturday, [Time] to [Time], and we're closed on Sundays. Leave a message and we'll call you back during our next open hours.

Weekend greeting with an on-call option

Holiday greetings

One-day holiday greeting

Happy holidays from [Business]. Our office is closed today for [holiday]. We'll be back on [Day], [Date] at [Time]. Please leave a message and we'll call you back as soon as we return. Thanks, and enjoy the day!

Closed for several days

Holiday greeting with an emergency line

Thanks for calling [Business]. We're closed today for [holiday]. If you have a life-threatening emergency, hang up and call 911. For urgent issues, call our emergency line at [Phone]. For everything else, leave a message and we'll call you back on [Day].

Vacation or team break

Hi, this is [Business]. Our team is on a short break, and the office is closed until [Date]. Please leave a message. When we're back, we'll return calls in the order we got them. Thanks for your patience!

For anything else, leave a message and we'll call you back during business hours.

Voicemail, answering service or AI receptionist?

A voicemail greeting is free and takes five minutes to record. The trade-off is that every caller has to wait for a callback, and some will call someone else instead.

Having a person answer costs more. A full-time receptionist earns a median of $38,010 a year, or $18.27 an hour, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. One person also can't cover every night and weekend.

An AI receptionist sits in between. It can answer the call at any hour, answer common questions and book appointments. For real prices, see our AI receptionist cost guide.

Tips and mistakes to avoid

  • Forgetting to change your holiday greeting back. Set a calendar reminder for the day you reopen.
  • Promising a callback time you can't keep. If you say "by 10 a.m.," make sure someone actually calls by then.
  • Rambling. Long greetings make people hang up before the beep.
  • Leaving out the 911 line. If you run any kind of emergency line, always tell callers with a life-threatening emergency to call 911 first.
  • Asking for private details. Clinics and law firms shouldn't ask callers to leave medical or case details by text.
  • Letting your mailbox fill up. A full mailbox means callers can't leave a message at all. Clear it every morning.

FAQ

What should an after-hours voicemail greeting say?

Say your business name, that you're closed, your hours and when you'll call back. If you offer emergency service, say how to reach the on-call line, and tell callers with a life-threatening emergency to hang up and call 911.

How long should an after-hours voicemail greeting be?

Aim for about 20 to 30 seconds. Put the most important details first, like your name, your hours and any emergency instructions.

Should I send a text after a missed after-hours call?

Yes, if your phone system can text from your business number. Many callers won't leave a voicemail, and a quick text lets them reply or book without waiting for morning. Only text people who called you, and don't ask for private medical or legal details by text.

Do I need a separate voicemail greeting for holidays?

Yes. Update your greeting before each holiday with the exact days you're closed and the day you'll start returning calls. Set a reminder to switch it back when you reopen.

Is an AI receptionist better than after-hours voicemail?

An AI receptionist can answer the call, answer common questions and book an appointment, so the caller gets help right away. Voicemail is cheaper and simpler, but the caller has to wait for a callback. Our AI receptionist cost guide compares prices.
