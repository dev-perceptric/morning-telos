What does an AI automation agency do?

An AI automation agency finds the repetitive work in a business and builds systems that handle it. That usually means connecting the tools a company already uses and adding AI where it does the job better than a fixed rule.

Here is what a good AI automation agency works on:

  • Finding the right work to automate: It maps how your team spends its week and ranks tasks by hours saved and risk.
  • Workflow automation: It moves data between your CRM, email, accounting and other tools so nobody copies and pastes.
  • AI agents: It builds agents that read, decide and act, like answering customers, qualifying leads or processing documents.
  • Custom software: When off-the-shelf tools don't fit, it builds internal tools, portals and dashboards.
  • Running it after launch: It monitors every system, fixes what breaks and adds the next automation.

What makes a good AI automation agency?

Most software and marketing agencies now list AI automation as a service, so the label alone won't tell you much. Here's what to look for:

  • Real case studies with numbers: Look for named clients and measured results, like hours saved or reply rates. Be careful with projections presented as results.
  • They start with your goal: A good agency asks what outcome you want before recommending tools.
  • They build on your current tools: Your team shouldn't have to switch systems to benefit.
  • They stay after launch: Automations break when tools change. Ask who fixes them and how fast.
  • Clear pricing: You should know the project price and monthly fee before you sign.

How to choose an AI automation agency

Use these questions on your first call with any agency:

  1. What outcome will we measure, and how?
  2. Can you show a case study from a business like ours, with real numbers?
  3. Which of our current tools will you connect to?
  4. Who owns the systems and the data when the project ends?
  5. What does it cost to build, and what does it cost each month after launch?
  6. What happens when something breaks, and how fast do you fix it?
  7. When would you tell us to buy software instead of building something custom?

FAQ

What is the best AI automation agency?

It depends on your size and goal. Small and mid-size businesses that want one partner for strategy, software and AI tend to fit Morning Telos, LeftClick or LowCode Agency. Enterprises that need a large transformation program tend to fit Tenex, HatchWorks AI or Markovate.

How much does an AI automation agency cost?

Published prices on this list range from about $3,000 a month for an embedded engineer (AY Automate) to $5,000–$50,000 per fixed-price project (LeftClick) and $9,000–$20,000+ for apps and AI agents (LowCode Agency). Most agencies also charge a monthly fee to monitor and improve what they build.

What does an AI automation agency do?

It finds the repetitive work in your business, then builds AI agents, workflow automations and software that handle it, usually connected to the tools you already use. Good agencies also stay after launch to fix and improve those systems.

Should I hire an AI automation agency or use software?

Start with off-the-shelf software when your need is simple and common. Hire an agency when the work spans several systems, your process is unusual, or you bought a tool and never got it working.

How long does an AI automation project take?

Simple automations often go live in days. Larger systems with several tools and AI agents usually take a few weeks, and enterprise programs can run for months.
