> **Short answer:** An AI receptionist costs about **$25 to $500 a month** for most small businesses. Plans are priced by calls, minutes or callers, and extra usage is billed on top. Human answering services start around **$250 to $300 a month** for a small number of calls, and an in-house receptionist costs a median **$38,010 a year** in pay alone. Custom AI receptionists cost **$5,000 to $25,000** to build.

These are estimates from published plans as of November 2026. Your real cost depends on call length, repeat callers and any add-ons.

## What changes the price

- **Call volume and length:** The biggest factor. Long calls hurt on per-minute plans, and many short calls hurt on per-call plans.
- **Pricing unit:** Calls, minutes, unique callers and conversations are all counted differently. Compare at your real volume.
- **Overage rates:** Going over your plan can cost $0.15 to $3 per call or $0.25 to $0.50 per minute on AI apps.
- **Integrations:** Booking into your calendar, CRM or practice software may need a higher plan or setup work.
- **Phone costs:** Voice AI platforms like Retell AI and Bland AI bill phone lines and numbers separately.
- **Human backup:** Hybrid plans that hand calls to a live person cost more than AI-only plans.

## AI receptionist vs human answering service vs in-house

| Option | Typical monthly cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| AI receptionist app | ~$25 to $500 | Most small businesses: answering, FAQs, booking and message taking around the clock |
| Human answering service | ~$250 to $2,100+ | Calls that need a person's judgment, at lower volume |
| In-house receptionist | ~$3,170+ in pay alone ($38,010 a year median, before benefits) | Busy offices that need someone on site |
| Custom AI receptionist | $5,000 to $25,000 to build, plus a monthly fee | Multi-location or high-volume businesses with unique rules and systems |

## Buy an app or build custom?

For most small businesses, an AI receptionist app is the right start. A custom build makes sense when the receptionist has to book into your own software, follow rules no app supports, or handle enough calls that per-call fees add up.
